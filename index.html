Biography

is a PhD candidate at the Institute of Information Security, ETH Zürich, advised by Prof. Dr. David Basin and Assoc. Prof. Dr. Srđan Krstić. His research combines formal methods and model-driven engineering to build software systems that are secure by design, with an emphasis on provable compliance with data-protection regulations such as the GDPR. Before ETH, he earned an MSc in Formal Methods from the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid and a BSc in Computer Science from the Vietnamese-German University.
