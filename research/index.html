Research Interests

My research lies at the intersection of formal methods, software engineering, and security & privacy. I build software engineering methodologies, using formal methods, with the goal of improving the security and privacy of software systems.

Model-driven engineering (MDE) is a software development methodology that puts models at the centre of the development process. In my work, MDE promotes security and privacy by design. Modelling security and privacy policies makes them precise, well-documented, and amenable to automatic analysis. With the appropriate model transformations in place, these models can further be compiled into an enforcement mechanism.

Some of the data protection principles

  • Lawfulness, fairness & transparency — personal data is processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner. GDPR Art. 5(1)(a)
  • Purpose limitation — personal data is used only for the purposes for which it was collected. Art. 5(1)(b)
  • Data minimisation — only the personal data necessary for a purpose is collected. Art. 5(1)(c)
  • Accuracy — personal data is kept accurate and, where needed, up to date. Art. 5(1)(d)
  • Storage limitation — personal data is kept only as long as the purpose requires. Art. 5(1)(e)
  • Integrity & confidentiality — personal data is protected against unauthorised access, loss, or damage. Art. 5(1)(f)
  • Accountability — the controller can demonstrate compliance with these principles. Art. 5(2)
  • Lawful basis & consent — processing requires a lawful basis; consent must be freely given and specific. Art. 6(1)(a), 7
  • Data subject rights — access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, and objection. Art. 15–21
  • Data protection by design & by default — protections are built into systems from the outset. Art. 25

Some directions

Securing data at the source

Databases are where sensitive data ultimately stores, so access control policies are best enforced there, i.e., at the source. I develop model-driven techniques and tools that rewrites SQL queries into security- and privacy-aware stored procedures that enforces data protetion and fine-grained access-control policies.

Privacy-aware programming/modeling languages

Enforcement should not stop at the database. I am interested in programming languages that treat privacy as a first-class concern, where privacy policies are expressed in the language itself and the compiler or runtime guarantees that programs cannot violate them.

Fighting online scams

Beyond the lab, I work on protecting ordinary users from online fraud. I am interested in how software engineering, security, and public education can be combined to detect scams and raise digital-safety awareness across Vietnam and the wider region.
