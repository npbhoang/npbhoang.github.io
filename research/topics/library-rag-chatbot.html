A Retrieval-Augmented Chatbot for Library Catalog Search

Open Bachelor's thesis · RAG, Information retrieval, LLMs

Motivation

Libraries make their collections searchable through an online public access catalog (OPAC). Such catalogs are typically built on structured bibliographic records, e.g., in the MARC 21 format [1], and offer keyword-based searches over fields such as title, author, and subject. Visitors, however, rarely know the exact title or author of what they are looking for. They describe a need in natural language (“an introductory book on machine learning for beginners”), and they expect help in the way a librarian would provide it.

Keyword search handles such requests poorly. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) [2] addresses this by first retrieving relevant documents from a knowledge source (in this case, the library catalog) and then generating an answer grounded in the retrieved documents.
