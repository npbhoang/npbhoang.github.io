Personal Data Analysis of Website Privacy Notices

Open Bachelor's thesis · Graph theory, Data protection

Motivation

Privacy policies are the main source of information about how a website handles personal data: which data it collects, for what purposes, with whom it shares the data, and how long the data is retained. Data protection laws, such as the GDPR [2] and the Vietnamese Law on Personal Data Protection [3], require organizations to provide this information transparently.

In practice, however, privacy policies are long, written in legal language, and often vague (e.g., “we may share your information with our partners”). Very few users actually read them. Moreover, a user may have accounts on dozens of websites yet have no overview of which organizations hold their personal data, e.g., their phone number or location, and to which third parties these data are passed on.

Research tools, such as PoliGraph [1], already extract data collection statements from privacy policies and represent them as a knowledge graph. These tools can be used by website users who want to explore and compare the policies of the websites they use.
